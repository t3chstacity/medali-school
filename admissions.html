Admissions & Enrollment

We welcome applications for Playgroup, Pre-Primary, Primary, and Junior School throughout the academic year.

4-Step Admission Process

1

Submit Inquiry / Application

Complete the online admission form or pick up an application pack at the school office.

2

Campus Visit & Learner Assessment

Tour our facilities and complete a friendly grade-placement interview.

3

Admission Offer & Fee Structure

Receive your official admission letter, uniform list, and termly fee schedule.

4

Registration & Orientation

Submit required documents and welcome your child to The Medali School family.

Required Documents

  • Copy of Child's Birth Certificate
  • 2 Recent Passport-Size Photographs
  • Previous School Report Card / Transfer Letter
  • NEMIS / Assessment Number (for Grade 1–9 transfers)
  • Copy of Parent/Guardian National ID
